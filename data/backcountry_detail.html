Why We Like The TX Hike Mid GTX Hiking Boot

We've got a lot of ground to cover—and it's not easy ground either. So we need the control and comfort offered by La Sportiva's TX Hike Mid GTX Hiking Boot. It has a specialized midsole that levels our ride by stiffening the side-to-side flex without sacrificing the comfortable cushioning it offers. Plus, its GORE-TEX membrane lends loads of aeration and waterproofing so our feet stay fresh on the journey up those jagged peaks while a Vibram Mega Grip outsole clasps our feet to the ground like magnets on metal.

Defective Boots

I gave it as a gift but have feedback to share

I am profoundly disappointed with the quality of these La Sportiva shoes, which were received in the same order as the defective Black Diamond trekking poles. This is the second item in a single order that arrived clearly defective. Immediately upon unboxing, I noticed a severe defect on the outsole. The rubber sole, particularly within the lug pattern (tread), clearly shows bubbling (blisters/bulges). This is not merely a cosmetic issue but a critical manufacturing flaw that severely compromises the shoes' durability, waterproofness, and safety. A sole with this defect is highly likely to delaminate or fail quickly on the trail. Overall Conclusion regarding Backcountry.com Purchase: Receiving two defective items (these shoes and the trekking poles) in one order points to serious issues with quality control and inventory management at Backcountry.com. Selling items of this quality at full price is unacceptable. I demand an immediate refund or new and defect-free product, along with adequate compensation for the significant inconvenience caused.

These boots are a great value! Waterproof. Tough and [relatively] light weight. Tough lugged sole, but plenty of flex. Learn more about TX Hike Mid GTX Hiking Boot - Men's

GORE-TEX is a high-performance fabric that is both waterproof and breathable. This means it keeps water out while allowing sweat and moisture to escape. When you wear GORE-TEX clothing or footwear, you stay dry and comfortable, even during intense activities like hiking in wet conditions.

Vibram Mega-Grip is a specialized rubber outsole designed to provide superior traction on various surfaces, especially in wet or slippery conditions. This feature helps you maintain stability and grip, reducing the risk of slips and falls on uneven terrain, making your hiking experience safer and more enjoyable.

MEMlex is a proprietary material used in the midsole of hiking shoes that provides excellent cushioning and support. This means that your feet will feel more comfortable during long hikes, reducing fatigue and allowing you to enjoy your outdoor adventures for longer periods.

The STB Control System is a technology that stiffens the midsole of hiking footwear, providing better support and control on rough or uneven ground. This feature helps you navigate challenging terrains with ease, reducing the risk of twisting an ankle and improving your overall stability while hiking.

The Traverse Last is a specific mold used to shape hiking shoes, designed to provide a comfortable fit, especially in the forefoot area. A well-designed last enhances stability and comfort, which is crucial for outdoor activities. This means you can hike longer distances without discomfort, allowing you to focus on enjoying nature.
